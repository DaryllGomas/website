What this assessment does not cover

The highest-impact risks are not on this network. Account takeover, credential reuse, and SIM-swap attacks bypass every control measured above, because they never touch the perimeter. They cannot be tested remotely and must be verified by hand.

The following require manual confirmation each cycle:

Breach exposure checked for all primary email addresses
Password manager audit clear of reused, weak, and compromised entries
Active sessions and third-party app authorisations reviewed and revoked
Phishing-resistant 2FA (hardware key or passkey) on all critical accounts — and the backup key tested
Mobile carrier port-out lock in place
Data-broker listings re-checked — they repopulate
Backup restored from the offline copy — an untested backup is a hypothesis
